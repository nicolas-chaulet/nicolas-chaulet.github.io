I am technology leader with a passion for buildings and sustainability. 

In the last 4 years as the CTO of CANOA I have been committed to tackle the climate emergency by building tools that foster circularity within interior design. 

After completed my PhD, I started my journey in the tech for building design started at Buro Happold Engineering more than 10 years ago, where I focused on enabling people centered design through fast and intuitive analysis tools. I then joined flux.io that became HELIX RE, where I setup the London office and led the AI research and development team that automated the digitization of indoor spaces. During that time, I also founded Principia Labs, a company that enabled research teams to build on the latest development in geometric Deep Learning.‍

I now live in Boston with my wife and kids.
